# Standard Operating Procedure of TOBI
This is a comprehensive checklist for TOBI, a custom avionics flight computer used for HPR (high power rocketry) model rockets. This device is for logging and saving instrument data only, not pyrotechnic parachute deployment. 
## Pre-launch Items:
- Custom Avionics bay
- PCB (ESP, DSO32, barometer)
- LiPo batteries
- USBC cable
- Laptop
- Electronics screwdriver
- Electrical tape

Items to test before launch day:
- Device activates
  - TOBI correctly allows arming device
  - Powers on WiFI & users can connect
  - Live data is running
  - Data rate values are correct
  - TOBI fully runs startup without errors
    - TOBI ensures battery voltage ensure LiPos are charged
- Assembly
  - Ensure no parts on PCB are loose
  - Tighten screw terminals
  - WiFi can be accessed through body tube
- Ensure packing for transit will not cause any items on device to come loose

## Launch Day Procedure:
- Check battery voltages
- Ensure TOBI powers on
  - Ensure live data is running properly
- Lock and load device into ebay
  - Ensure screws are tightened
  - 
### Pad Operations 
**DO NOT ARM DEVICE UNTIL ON PAD**
- Check WiFi Operations
- Once the rocket is on the rails and ready to launch after hooking up the motors, then arm the device for flight

## Recovery
- Ensure all data is downloaded to the user's device before shutting down electronics
- Double-check both CSV files have been downloaded
