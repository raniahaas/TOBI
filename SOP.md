Majority of my inspiration came from previous use with the Featherweight Altimeter Blueraven App as well as another app called Euristiq. A fun easter egg in regards to the coloring is the use of Gunship Grey (676C73), which is the paint color used on the B-2 Spirit, which is the plane that got be re-introduced into aviation and rocketry. Overall color pallet consists of 6 colors: #C83408, #F5ECD3, #FB961B, #191553, #1E2719, and #676C73.

### Final PCB Design
For the most part, final design went smoothly--biggest issues arrived after the DRC and when finally sending the gerber files to JLC PCB. Mainly, the DRC had a lot issues regarding: clearance violations, items shorting, thermal relief connection to zone incomplete, front/back solder mask aperture bridges items with different nets. Clearance and shorting were both easy fixes, required some more moveing around parts and chaning the overall nets. However, the thermal relief and solder mask aperture were a little more difficult to understand and fix. They did end up being simple fixes like editing the number of thermal relief holes and adding extra vias to help connect ground points. Additionally, when uploading to JLC I learned you have to use mounting holes rather than edge cuts as when uploading the gerber file it then sees that as parts rather than a board with cuts.
